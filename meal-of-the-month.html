Marinated Tomatoes & Charred Okra over Polenta

Late-summer produce, a bright marinade, and creamy polenta come together in a bowl that feels generous without being complicated.

Glazed chicken is an optional protein

Build the bowl.

The vegetables and polenta are the core recipe. Serve them on their own, or add your preferred protein when you want a more substantial meal.

  • 1 cup stone-ground polenta
  • 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth or water
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 pound fresh okra, halved lengthwise
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper, optional
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil or parsley
  • To taste black pepper and salt
  • Optional glazed chicken or another protein

1. Marinate the tomatoes. Toss the tomatoes and red onion with vinegar, one tablespoon of olive oil, garlic, red pepper, black pepper, and a small pinch of salt. Let them rest while the rest of the dish cooks.

2. Cook the polenta. Bring the broth or water to a gentle boil. Slowly whisk in the polenta, reduce the heat, and cook until creamy, stirring often according to the package timing.

3. Char the okra. Toss the okra with the remaining oil and black pepper. Place cut-side down in a hot skillet and cook without moving until deeply browned. Turn and finish until tender.

4. Finish and serve. Fold the herbs into the tomatoes. Spoon polenta into bowls, add the charred okra, and finish with the tomato mixture and its juices. Add the optional protein last.

Tomatoes provide vitamin C and lycopene, while okra contributes fiber and folate. Serving them over polenta makes the meal satisfying and adaptable. Using low-sodium broth and controlling added salt keeps the flavor focused on the vegetables, acid, herbs, and char.

This is food-as-medicine in a practical form: recognizable ingredients, flexible preparation, and a meal people can recreate after the Chat & Chew.
